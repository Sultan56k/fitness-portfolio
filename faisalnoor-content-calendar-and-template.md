# Fit with Faisal Noor — 30-Day Content Calendar & Writer Template

## How this calendar works

Every article is tagged with:
- **Funnel bucket** — which service it should ultimately push toward
- **CTA target** — the exact button/link to use
- **Search intent** — why someone would actually search this (not "trending," but real problem-aware intent)

Cadence: **4 articles/week** (Mon/Wed/Fri/Sun or whatever fits) for the first 30 days — real depth beats raw volume. Once you and your writer have the template down cold, you can scale toward daily.

Funnel buckets used:
- **DIET** → Diet Coaching plans (Rs. 6,000–15,000)
- **STRENGTH** → Strength Training Course (Rs. 4,500–17,500)
- **CONSULT** → 1-on-1 Consultancy sessions (Rs. 1,500–4,500) — lowest-friction CTA, good for top-of-funnel articles
- **APP** → FitLife app download — good for calculator/tracking-related topics

## Reusable Article Template (hand this to your writer)

Every article follows this structure. Fill in the bracketed sections.

```
TITLE: [Problem-focused, specific — avoid vague titles like "Fitness Tips"]
TARGET KEYWORD: [main search phrase]
FUNNEL BUCKET: [DIET / STRENGTH / CONSULT / APP]
WORD COUNT TARGET: 900–1400 words

---

1. HOOK (50–100 words)
Open with the specific problem the reader has RIGHT NOW. Not a generic
intro ("Fitness is important..."). Name the frustration directly.
Example: "You've cut calories, you're eating clean, and the scale hasn't
moved in three weeks. Here's what's actually happening."

2. WHY THIS HAPPENS (150–250 words)
Explain the underlying cause with real substance — not fluff. This is
where Faisal's credibility shows: cite the mechanism (hormonal, metabolic,
behavioral), not just "everyone's different."

3. WHAT MOST PEOPLE GET WRONG (150–250 words)
Call out the common mistake or bad advice circulating online. This
positions Faisal as someone who cuts through noise — his differentiator.

4. THE FIX / FRAMEWORK (300–500 words)
The actual practical guidance. Use his "build it like a system" angle
where relevant — step-by-step, structured, no vague platitudes.
Use subheadings, short paragraphs, bullet points for scannability.

5. LOCAL/PRACTICAL TOUCH (optional but valuable, 100–150 words)
Where relevant, tie to Pakistani food availability, local gym access,
budget realities — this is a real differentiator vs global fitness blogs.

6. SOFT CTA (50–100 words)
Never hard-sell. Frame it as the natural next step for someone who
wants this personalized. Match the CTA to the FUNNEL BUCKET:

  - DIET bucket:
    "If you want a diet plan built around your actual routine and food
    availability — not a generic template — [start with a Diet Coaching
    plan]."

  - STRENGTH bucket:
    "If you want form checks and a structured plan instead of guessing —
    [check out the Strength Training Course]."

  - CONSULT bucket (use for top-of-funnel / broad topics):
    "Still not sure what's actually stalling your progress? A single
    [Quick Chat session] can usually pinpoint it in 15 minutes."

  - APP bucket:
    "Want to track this without a spreadsheet? [FitLife] has this
    calculator built in, free on iOS."

---

STYLE NOTES FOR WRITER:
- Write like Faisal is talking to one person, not a crowd — "you," not "one"
- Short sentences. Avoid fitness-blog cliché phrases ("crush your goals",
  "unlock your potential")
- Back claims with actual reasoning, not just assertions — this is a
  YMYL-sensitive niche, credibility matters for both readers and Google
- Every article needs ONE clear takeaway a reader could act on today
- No medical claims — always frame as coaching guidance, not medical advice
  (matches the disclaimer already on the pricing page)
```

## Quick notes on execution

- **Batch by bucket, not by day** — write 3-4 DIET articles in one sitting, then 3-4 STRENGTH, etc. Faster for a writer to stay in one expertise mode.
- **Internal links**: every new article should link back to at least 1-2 earlier articles in the same bucket, and to the relevant service page. This builds topical authority and keeps visitors moving toward booking.
- **Track what converts**: after 30 days, check which bucket drives the most Calendly bookings — double down there before scaling volume further.
